Risen Goliath

BOSS
Colossus type Stratum VII+ Physical / Void
First seenStratum VII
Base HP61.0B
ResistancesPhys 40%
WeaknessVoid

A hulking revenant reassembled from the wreckage of an earlier expedition, the Risen Goliath guards the approach to the Sundered Vault. Its plating is fused with raw aether, letting it shrug off physical blows while its core remains vulnerable to Void damage.

During an engagement it channels Crush at low cadence — a heavy telegraphed strike that ignores a portion of Barrier. Attuning your barrier recharge before the cast lands is the difference between a clean pull and a reset.
