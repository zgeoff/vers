Respite Style Probes

The running record of finding Respite's look, newest round first. All shots are in-engine: WebGPU, TSL bloom, instanced massing — the shipping stack. The palette round that preceded these lives in the Respite Anchor Sheet artifact. Settled so far: dark and warm beats daylight; the A×C color world (sodium warmth at street level, teal/violet signals above); the composition is a town square seen from its open end, deliberately irregular; market and avatar silhouettes chosen.

Round 25 · wisps, not sheets

resolved

The fix for "adjusting opacity rather than life": structure in motion. Each fog layer's noise is pushed through a threshold so it breaks into separate shapes, and the sampling coordinate is warped by a second noise so those shapes curl and evolve rather than slide rigidly. The banks gained a fast-scudding fine octave over the slow body; the mist became two stacked layers rolling at different speeds; the gate haze streams upward at double speed and pulses; and the first motion elements landed early — ragged smoke columns off the rooftop machinery, solid at the vent mouth and gone by the top.

Verdict: "it needs tuning, but that's a good start." Direction adopted — wisp structure stays; fine values remain knob work for the settle-and-tune round.

Round 22 · the tuner — armed with technology

resolved

Round 21's real verdict wasn't about amplitudes — it was that the loop itself was wrong: the machine kept guessing at taste it doesn't have. This round ships the control layer instead of another guess. Every constant in every surface recipe is now a live knob: panel cells and amplitudes, wear scales and fade bands, course-shadow heights and depths, grain, clamps, per-building roughness — plus the light rig (ambient, bounce, key, lamps, gate glow), the grade (vignette, warmth, bloom), and the grounding occlusion. Knobs are TSL uniforms, so sliders edit the material graph in place at frame rate. copy json exports the whole configuration for baking — the exact workflow that settled the layout, applied to surfaces.

Roadmap note agreed alongside: the broad-strokes phases continue next — lighting cohesion, then atmosphere, then motion — and the deep texture exploration moves to the final-fidelity phase, where one pilot building goes ground-up through every pass with these tools in hand.

Verdict: "sliders are great, can iterate deeper later." Tuner adopted as standing infrastructure; deep texture iteration deferred to the final-fidelity phase per the agreed roadmap. On to the unification passes.

Round 21 · recipes, not wallpaper

resolved

The lesson from rounds 19 and 20, applied: neither tool was wrong — uniform application was. Each building now composes organic wear and quantized paneling selectively, anchored to its own architecture, with areas of deliberate rest. After a first too-timid pass, amplitudes were tripled and ambient light lifted — which exposed the deeper truth: texture legibility is mostly a lighting problem. Albedo variation multiplied into a dim face is invisible at any strength; the recipes read exactly where light lands (the market's lamp-lit faces, the drums by their posts). The full fix is the upcoming lighting-cohesion pass — deliberate light ON the walls — not ever-higher contrast.

Verdict: "way too subtle" — and after the amplitude boost, the deeper one: "i don't think you're a good judge of textures. i think that requires human in the loop configuration/refinement, i just need to be armed with technology." Texture refinement is now human-in-the-loop by design; round 22 builds the arms, and the deep texture exploration itself moves to the final-fidelity phase.

Round 20 · cladding, not camouflage

resolved

Same idea, opposite execution: instead of smooth noise interpolating across faces, world space is quantized into cells and each cell holds one flat tone — the difference between blotch and paneling. Seam lines run between courses and joints; pavement gets large paver cells with crisp joints on both axes.

Verdict: "bricks at best, graph paper at worst" — uniform paneling rejected like uniform noise before it, with the key insight that both tools are usable selectively. That became round 21's per-building recipes.

Round 19 · surfaces break the flat-block read
